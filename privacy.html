1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you fill out the “Book a visit” form, you share your name, phone number, property address and the service you are interested in. If you call, text or talk with us in person, we may also keep notes about your property and the work you ask for.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, the company that hosts this site may automatically record basic technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you open. This is used to keep the site running and secure.

We do not ask for payment card details, Social Security numbers or other sensitive information through this website.

2. How we use it

  • To contact you about your request and schedule a free visit.
  • To give you a quote and a plan for your property.
  • To provide the services you hire us for, including recurring visits.
  • To send reminders and updates, like a text before we arrive.
  • To answer your questions and keep records of the work we do.
  • To keep this website working and protect it from misuse.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

3. Text messages

When you give us your phone number, you agree that we may call or text you about your request, your visits and your service. These are service messages, not marketing. Message and data rates may apply, and how often we text depends on your schedule.

You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP, or ask for help by replying HELP. We do not share or sell your mobile number or your consent to receive texts with third parties for their marketing purposes.

4. When we share it

We only share your information when it is needed to serve you or required by law:

  • Service providers that help us run the business, such as website hosting, messaging and scheduling tools. They may use your information only to provide those services to us.
  • Legal reasons, when required by law, a court order or to protect the rights and safety of our customers, our crew or the public.
  • Business changes, if Silver Hand is sold or merged; the new owner would have to respect this policy.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or social media pixels. Our fonts are served from our own website, not from a third party. The map in the “Local service” section opens Google Maps only if you click it; from that point, Google’s own privacy policy applies.

If we add analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy first.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to respond to you, provide our services and keep reasonable business and tax records. If you never become a customer, we delete your request when it is no longer useful. You can ask us to delete it sooner (see below).

7. Your choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what information we have about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or out of date;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it by law;
  • stop calling or texting you.

Depending on where you live, including Maryland, state law may give you additional privacy rights. We will honor any request we are required to, and we will not treat you differently for making one.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information and limit who can see it. No website or online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we take it seriously and only collect what we need.

9. Children

This website is meant for adults arranging services for their homes. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Important changes will be easy to spot here before they take effect.

11. Contact us

For any question or request about your privacy, reach us through the form on our website or reply to any text message we have sent you.
